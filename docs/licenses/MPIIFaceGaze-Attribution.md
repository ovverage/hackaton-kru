# MPIIFaceGaze: source and attribution

Dataset citation: Bulling, Andreas, 2023, **MPIIFaceGaze**, DaRUS, V1.
https://doi.org/10.18419/DARUS-3240

Paper: Xucong Zhang, Yusuke Sugano, Mario Fritz, Andreas Bulling.
**It’s Written All Over Your Face: Full-Face Appearance-Based Gaze Estimation.**
IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition Workshops (CVPRW),
2017, pp. 2299–2308. https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPRW.2017.284

The dataset builds on MPIIGaze. Its authors publish it under Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International and describe its use as
non-commercial scientific purposes. The complete license is retained alongside
this notice as `MPIIFaceGaze-CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.txt`.

Official dataset page: https://perceptualui.org/research/datasets/MPIIFaceGaze/

Official paper and original dataset description:
https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/departments/computer-vision-and-machine-learning/research/gaze-based-human-computer-interaction/its-written-all-over-your-face-full-face-appearance-based-gaze-estimation

Qorgau used the dataset for training its research gaze estimator. The generated
ONNX weights and model metadata are separate artifacts; this notice is not an
upstream model, a copy of the dataset, or a new grant of rights. The model manifest
identifies the bundled weights and their hashes. Training and conversion do not
by themselves establish a right to distribute the resulting artifacts.
